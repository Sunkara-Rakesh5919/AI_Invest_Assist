## 🤖 **AI SCORING METHODOLOGY**

```

    Technical Analysis × 0.80 +
    Fundamental Analysis × 0.60 +
    Sentiment Analysis × 0.50 +
    Volume Analysis × 0.70
) / 4

Technical Score = (RSI × 0.4 + MACD × 0.3 + Bollinger × 0.3) × 0.8
Fundamental Score = (P/E × 0.4 + P/B × 0.3 + ROE × 0.3) × 0.6
Sentiment Score = (News × 0.3 + Social × 0.3 + Analyst × 0.4) × 0.5
Volume Score = (Volume Trend) × 0.7
```
